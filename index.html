How it began

The Journey

From an audition project to a six-member group.

2023

The Debut: Dream Academy

A global audition program developed by HYBE and Geffen Records brought 20 contestants together to compete for a place in a new group.

Nov 2023

Six members revealed

The live finale announced Daniela, Lara, Manon, Megan, Sophia and Yoonchae as the final lineup.

2024

A new chapter

KATSEYE made their music debut with “Debut” and released their first EP, SIS (Soft Is Strong).
