How it began

The Journey

From a worldwide search to a six-member group—and the music chapters that followed.

2023

The Debut: Dream Academy

HYBE and Geffen Records developed a 12-week audition program to form a global girl group. More than 120,000 submissions led to 20 selected contestants. They trained and took part in three singing and dancing missions, evaluated by company experts and online fan votes; episodes appeared on YouTube, Weverse and ABEMA in Japan.

17 Nov 2023

The final six are revealed

After the competition narrowed the 20 contestants to 10 finalists, the live finale in Los Angeles revealed Daniela, Lara, Manon, Megan, Sophia and Yoonchae as KATSEYE. The group name and lineup marked the start of their shared chapter.

Jun 2024

First music release

KATSEYE introduced their sound with the single “Debut” in June. The release began the group’s public music career after the audition program.

Aug 2024

SIS (Soft Is Strong)

The debut EP expanded the first singles into a five-track release. Its title reflects “Soft Is Strong” and the sisterhood formed among members from different backgrounds.

2025

BEAUTIFUL CHAOS

The second EP brought a new chapter with singles “Gnarly” and “Gabriela.” KATSEYE’s official story notes that the EP reached No. 4 on the Billboard 200, their first Top 10 on that chart.

2026

WILD

The third EP opened another era for the group. Its track list includes “PINKY UP,” “Animal,” “Hootie Frutti,” “Bel Air” and “That Way.”

Timeline checked 30 October 2026. Based on KATSEYE’s official story, Netflix Tudum’s Dream Academy overview and the official releases archive.
